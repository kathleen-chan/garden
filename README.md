# Oubaitori 🌱

Welcome to **Oubaitori**, a cozy and interactive virtual gardening experience where you can create and grow your own little worlds!

## Features

- **World Creation:** Create and name your own virtual worlds.
- **Multiple Biomes:** Explore different environments and switch between biomes.
- **World Management:** View and manage the worlds you've created.

## How to Play

1. Visit the [game website](https://kathleen-chan.github.io/garden/garden).
2. Click **Enter** to begin.
3. Navigate to the world creation section.
4. Enter a name for your new world.
5. Click **Create** to start your world.
6. Explore different biomes and grow your world!
7. Return to your worlds whenever you want to continue exploring.
